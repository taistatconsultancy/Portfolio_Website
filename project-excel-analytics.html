Excel Analytics Solutions

Advanced Excel dashboards and data models for reporting and decision support — turning messy spreadsheets into clean, reusable business tools.

Microsoft Excel PivotTables Power Query Dashboards

The Problem

Many teams rely on Excel for reporting but struggle with slow, error-prone workbooks, unclear structure, and no single view of key metrics. Data is often copied manually between sheets, and there's no easy way to slice by period, region, or product without rebuilding the report.

Leadership needed clear, repeatable dashboards that could be updated with new data without constant rework or relying on a data analyst every month.

What I Built

I designed and built Excel-based solutions that turn raw data into structured reports and dashboards. The workbooks are built for reuse — when new data is added, the user refreshes or pastes into the data sheet, and all dashboards and PivotTables update automatically.

I used dynamic arrays, named ranges, and conditional formatting so that KPIs and exceptions are visible at a glance, and added Power Pivot data models where multiple tables needed to be combined for ratio and trend measures.

1
Data Layer Single data sheet or external connection — one place to paste or refresh
2
Power Query / Data Model Transform, clean, and combine tables with measures and calculated columns
3
PivotTables & Calculated Fields Structured summaries that update with the data — no manual rework
4
Dashboard & Slicers Charts, KPIs, conditional formatting, and filters for stakeholder use

Outcome

Teams have reliable, reusable Excel templates and dashboards that support monthly reporting and ad hoc analysis without heavy IT support. Decision-making is faster because the data is structured and the views are consistent.

  • Clean, reusable workbooks — refresh data and all views update
  • KPI summary views with variance vs. plan built in
  • Drill-down by time period, region, or product via slicers
  • Power Pivot models for multi-table ratio and trend analysis
  • Conditional formatting for instant exception spotting
  • Documented structure so non-technical staff can maintain files
