Problems I help fleets solve

Fuel loss & siphoning alerts

Match tank-level drops against geofences and stops to flag unexplained fuel drains, with a clear record of every incident.

Turnaround time (TAT) analytics

Measure depot queuing, loading, and offloading times so dispatch can plan better and trucks spend less time idle.

Driver safety scorecards

Speeding, harsh braking, over-revving, night driving, and VSS video alarms turned into scorecards for coaching and client safety (HSE) reviews.

Delivery reconciliation & exceptions

Compare trips with delivery records and turn late deliveries and unauthorised stops into documented, actionable incidents.

Automated compliance reporting

Daily, weekly, and monthly reports that support client contracts and regulatory tracking requirements, without manual exports.

Telematics & system integration

Wialon, MiX Telematics, and Howen VSS data combined through their APIs into one dashboard, web portal, or ERP your team already uses.
